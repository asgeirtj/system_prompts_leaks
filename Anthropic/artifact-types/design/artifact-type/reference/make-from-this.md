# Making something else from a canvas

A deck, an animation or a doc made from artboards. Read `project/canvas.json` and the artboards it is to be made from: the ones named or selected, else all on the page in view, in reading order (rows top to bottom, each left to right). An artboard is one part: its `title` (or file name) names it, its copy is the content, its look is the brief. Make the thing in its own type, never a picture of the canvas. Leave the artboards as they are: never move, resize or edit one for this.

Then hand it over on the canvas: add one entry to `notes` in `project/canvas.json`, under a key not already there, `"deck1":{"kind":"link","x":0,"y":680,"href":"https://claude.ai/…","title":"Spring menu deck"}`: `href` is the new artifact's link verbatim, `title` its name, and there is NO `text` (with `text` it becomes a plain sticky and the link is lost; only titles and stickies need one). It draws a 640×112 card that opens the artifact, and PNG and PDF exports leave it out. Put it on bare canvas under what it was made from: 120 px under that artboard, left edges level (made from a whole row: under the row's first artboard). If something stands there or a row below leaves it no room, go down its column to the first bare ground, 120 px under the last artboard there. Never right of the rightmost artboard: that is where the next artboard goes. It covers nothing: no artboard, name strip, title or note. On a canvas with pages give it that artboard's `page`. `linkKind` is slides | motion | doc | design, or left out. One card for each artifact; none for an artifact that already has one, none for one that has nothing to do with this canvas. No claude.ai link for what you made: no card. Send only the index (Revising, step 3: read it again right before the call, change only your key). Refused because someone saved meanwhile: Revising, step 4. Refused otherwise: say so and give the link in your reply. This is part of delivering what was asked, so do it without being asked.
