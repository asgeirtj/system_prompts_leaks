# Slide HTML

A deck is ordinary HTML and CSS on a fixed 1920×1080 canvas (1440×1080 in a 4:3 deck): one `<section>` per slide, directly in `<body>`, with shared rules and `--variables` in the deck's CSS. Slides reads a deck by browser rules and works each slide out to plain values. Write normal CSS: what this page doesn't name behaves as it does in a browser, except that margins add up rather than collapse.

**What won't survive.** Slides drops these and rarer properties. `→` is what to write instead; with no arrow, nothing replaces it.
- Stacking: `z-index`, `order` → source order; later elements paint on top, with the exceptions under **Slide** and **Containers**.
- Layout: `float`, `columns`, `display:table`, side-by-side `inline-block` → flex or grid. `vertical-align`, `align-content` → `align-items`. `position:fixed`, `sticky` → `absolute`. `width:fit-content` → `display:inline-block`; in a flex column, `align-self:flex-start`.
- Grid: `grid-template-areas`, `grid-area`, `grid-auto-*`, named lines → `grid-template-columns`, `grid-template-rows` and line numbers.
- Clipping: `clip-path`, `mask` → `border-radius` with `overflow:hidden`, or an `<svg>`. `overflow:auto`, `scroll`, `clip` → `hidden`, on a `<div>`.
- Fills: `url()` backgrounds, `background-size`, `background-position` → an `<img>`. `conic-gradient`, a second gradient or image, a sized radial, px stops outside `repeating-linear-gradient` → one plain gradient, or an `<svg>`. `object-position`. `object-fit` other than `cover`, `contain`.
- Borders: a second colour or style on one box. A `border` with no colour is black (shapes: the text colour). `outline` → `border`. One corner → `border-radius`.
- Transforms: `scale`, and `translate` in %, outside `@keyframes` → `transform`. In `@keyframes`, `translate`, `rotate` and `scale` replace the rule's `transform`, so write the whole `transform` in the frames. `transform` functions out of the order translate, rotate, scale, skew lose the whole `transform`. 3D functions. `transform-origin` on a still rotation. `drop-shadow()` → `box-shadow`.
- Text: `word-break`, `hyphens`, `text-wrap`, `text-overflow`, `-webkit-line-clamp` → size the box. `writing-mode` → rotate a pinned `<p>`. `font-variant`, `font-feature-settings` → `font-variant-numeric:tabular-nums`. `white-space` other than `normal`, `nowrap` → `<br>`. `text-indent`.
- Inside a line: a phrase keeps only colour, bold, italic and underline. A size, face, padding or pill → a block of its own.
- Lists: a rule on an `<li>` with a marker → style the `<ul>` or `<ol>`. `list-style` other than `none`; `none` makes the list and its lines plain boxes, so a screen reader hears no list: keep the markers on a real list.
- Tables: a cell keeps only `text-align`, `width`, `color`, `padding` and, in the header, `font-weight`. Other type styles → the `<table>`. Fills → `background` on `<tr>`, not `<thead>`. Cell borders, `border-collapse`, `border-spacing`: a table draws its own hairlines. Only the header row is bold: no `<b>`, or `<th>` in other rows.
- Selectors: attribute selectors, `:is()`, `:where()`, `:has()`, `::marker`, `::first-line`, `::first-letter`, nested rules → classes. `:hover`, `:focus`: nothing is interactive.
- At-rules: `@import`, `@supports`, `@container`, `@property` other than a count-up (styles.md). `@media` is read at the slide's size, and only `screen`, `all`, orientation and px `min-` or `max-` width and height apply. `prefers-reduced-motion:no-preference` applies for every viewer, and `reduce` for none.
- Also: `inherit`, `initial`, `unset` → the value itself. `transition`, except on a fragment or with `@starting-style`.
- Caps: a number past one stops at it. Declaration dropped: in `@keyframes`, `font-size` or `line-height` under its floor, too many tracks, stops or layers. `gap` 512, `padding` 256 (cells 64, one value per table), `font-size` 8–400, `line-height` 0.5–4× the size, `letter-spacing` −24 to 32px, `border` 16, `border-radius` 256 (a bigger flat circle → `<x-shape kind="ellipse">`), grid 24 tracks, gradient 2–8 stops, `box-shadow` and `text-shadow` 8 layers, shadow offset 64, blur 160 (text 64), `filter: blur()` 20, `skew()` ±60. `border`, shadows and `blur()` take px only.

**What Slides adds.** A counter the `<section>` increments is its place in the deck, so `section { counter-increment: page; }` and `content: counter(page)` make a page number. On a `<section>` or `<div>` a person can't select or edit it by hand; on a `<p>` they can retype it, which drops the line's classes. Other HTML tags, such as `header`, `figure`, `blockquote`, `h4`, `dl`, `pre`, `strong` and `code`, become plain boxes and text; a phrase with `display:block` becomes a line of its own.

**Document.** A deck has a title, a size (the skill's 4:3 paragraph gives the numbers), shared CSS and fonts: a Google Fonts css2 link and/or the user's own font files (fonts.md). Up to 4 families. A `body` rule sets the deck defaults: text inherits as in CSS, except `font-size` and `font-weight` never inherit into h1–h3 (tag defaults until set). An unknown `font-family` heals to the generic.

**Slide = `<section id data-section="…" data-transition="fade|push|magic" hidden>`.** Children flow in it; `position:absolute` pins a child to the slide instead. Pinned boxes stay on the canvas and, normally, inside the 128px margins (left+width ≤ 1792, top+height ≤ 952). As in a browser, `width` and `height` leave out padding and border, so set `box-sizing:border-box` on a box whose edges you work out. Later children paint on top, and all flow content paints as ONE layer where the first flow child sits: a pinned backdrop listed after the first flow child hides the flow text — list backdrops first. Inside a `position:relative` `<div>`, pinned children always paint over its flow content, so a decorative rule goes where nothing else sits, or is a flow child. `<aside>` = speaker notes (one per slide, last; takes no space). `hidden` = skipped. `data-transition` = how this slide leaves. `data-section` = a section of your outline starts here; its value is that section's one-sentence description (with `project/deck.json`, put it in `sections` instead).

**Text.** `<h1>` `<h2>` `<h3>` `<p>` (defaults 96/64/44/32px, weights 600/600/600/400, no default margins). Set `font-size` yourself: ≥24px everywhere, labels, cells, footers too. `<ul>`/`<ol>` of plain `<li>`; after its words an `<li>` can hold a `<ul>`/`<ol>` of sub-items, ≤9 levels (fixed markers; no `style` on it; `data-indent="N"` on an `<li>` puts it N levels further in, but a skipped level is a list with no item of its own to a screen reader, so step one level at a time); `<br>`. Inside a line: `<b>`, `<i>`, `<u>`, `<a href="https://…">`, or a `<span>` with a colour. A pill is a `<p>` with `background` + `padding` + `border-radius`. Text wraps only at spaces; a word wider than its box breaks mid-word, so size every box to its longest word: about 0.6 × `font-size` per character plus padding. Opt-ins: `white-space:nowrap` keeps one line (a flow box grows; a pinned box's text runs past its edge); `min-width:min-content` or `max-content` on a FLOW text element or column keeps it at least as wide as its longest word or line (siblings shrink).

**Containers.** `position:relative` on a `<div>` lets `position:absolute` children pin to it (≤24 a host; split across sibling hosts). A pinned `<div>` in a host is flattened: its pinned children re-pin to the host (exact for `left`/`top`; `right`/`bottom`/centering need its size set, else error); it stays only if painted or holding flow content. A `%` on a pinned element needs a host of known size, else it's an error. A host with no flow child needs width and height. Under `align-items:center` an unsized host hugs its flow content.

**Images.** `<img src alt style="width; height; object-fit:cover|contain; border-radius">`; `alt` = what it shows (`alt=""` if decorative); `src` = an image file (png/jpg/webp/gif/svg; images.md). `data-crop="ZOOM X% Y%"` (zoom 1–50, object-position) is a person's crop: keep it unless `src` changes. A video clip: images.md.

**Graphics & live embeds.** Charts, illustrations and a diagram's curved lines (diagrams.md) are `<svg …>…</svg>`: one opaque graphic — paths, shapes, gradients, filters, local `#id` refs only. Skip `<text>`: fonts never load in an svg, so labels are `<p>`s over it. No script, on*=, foreignObject or URLs (`<style>`, animation, comments, CDATA: fine). ≤52 KB each; `width`/`height` = the `viewBox`; shown as an image. Entities: only numeric (`&#160;`), the XML five (`&` as `&amp;`); other characters literal (`—`). Prefer presentation attributes or `style=`: a drawing with a `<style>` is saved as an image.

A live embed, `<x-embed style="position:absolute; left; top; width; height">…a whole small page…</x-embed>`, is a sandboxed iframe (`allow-scripts`; CSP: no network, `font-src data:` only). Its HTML/CSS/JS runs, but the deck's `@font-face` and styles do NOT reach it (use a system font stack); its background is transparent until painted; viewers cannot click or type into it. A DIRECT child of `<section>`, pinned (left/top or right/bottom + width/height); ≤16 KB each, ≤8 per slide. Slides does not read its contents, so keep its text ≥24px. PPTX/PDF flatten it; the web page runs it. A heavy embed can crash Present on iPhone: use one canvas. In an embed, use CSS `@keyframes`, not SMIL: SMIL can stand still until its first loop ends.

**Tables.** `<table>` of `<tr>` with `<th>`/`<td>` (plain text; ≤100 rows × 24 columns); a first `<th>` row is the header. Column widths: `width:N%` on EVERY cell of the first row (any missing = equal columns). `text-align` on a cell aligns its column; `color` works on a cell; `font-family`, `font-size` and `color` go on the `<table>`, or inherit. Cells pad 0.35em 0.6em unless a cell sets `padding` (one per table); never `padding:… 0` on a text cell: text would sit on the rule. Every row must fit under the heading: else 24–28px or split the table. Columns never grow for one long word: make each column as wide as its longest word; 5+ columns at 32px in half a slide do not fit.

**Table banding.** A `<tr>` may carry a colour `background`; no background on cells, no rowspan (a `colspan` becomes empty cells); rows are as tall as their content (a height on a table is ignored). Merged group headers: a painted flex row above the table whose parts use the columns' `width:N%`, summing to 100. Row groups: a pinned painted spine beside the table. Do not rotate text inside a cell (45° labels: diagrams.md).

**Shapes & icons.** A painted `<div>` IS a rectangle; `<hr>` is a line (`border-top` = its stroke and thickness, else `color`). `<x-shape kind="ellipse|diamond|arrow-right|arrow-left|arrow-up|arrow-down|line">` is styled in CSS with `background`, `border` (the stroke), `box-shadow`, `opacity`, `width` and `height`; each kind fills its box, so an arrow is a block glyph whose head is the last 40% of its length: keep it near 2:1. Connectors: `<x-connector x1 y1 x2 y2 head="end|both|none" head-start|head-end="none|open|filled" route="straight|hv|vh|elbow">` is styled in CSS with `color`, `border-width` (2px default, ≤32) and `border-style:dashed|dotted`. It is a line with constant-size heads (`hv` = across then down, `vh` = down then across, `elbow` = 3 segments), in canvas coordinates in a `<section>` or relative to a `position:relative` `<div>` (px or %). A pinned layer (counts toward the 24); no `left`/`top`/`width`/`height`, no fill; a missing coordinate errors. Without coordinates it is a sized flow child (`width:96px` between cards) whose line runs corner to corner from `from="tl|tr|bl|br"` (default `tl`; a flat box: a straight arrow) (diagrams.md). **Motion (Present only).** SKILL.md says how. Never flash over three times a second, even if asked. A two-keyframe CSS one-shot of 100–333 ms plays in 334 ms. A loop that fades needs 333 ms per step or it stands still, and unless it's `alternate` the jump back is a step, so `from`/`to` needs 666 ms. In a drawing any `<animate>` counts as fading. Keyframes with opacity or colour can't overshoot (a `cubic-bezier` past 0–1). Move or turn anything large or high-contrast slowly; a small spinner may go faster. Stage arrivals with `both`, `animation-delay:calc(var(--i) * 120ms)` and `--i` on each element. A negative delay starts a loop part-way. A loop can't start at a click: put it inside the fragment, where it runs unseen until then. Animate a drawing's parts in its inline `<svg>`, not an `<x-embed>`, and put dots on a curve there: boxes can't follow a path. Use `@keyframes`, or SMIL: `<animate>`, `<set>`, `<animateMotion>`, and `<animateTransform>` that turns or moves. Slides checks `<animate>` for flashing: give it a `dur` in s or ms and a single time in `begin`, with `to`, `from` and `to`, or `values`, and `<set>` a single `begin`. If one `<animate>` flashes, `<set>`s make over six changes in a second, or Slides can't read some SMIL, such as a `calcMode` or a scaling `<animateTransform>`, all that drawing's SMIL stops. A still, PDF, PowerPoint and Reduce Motion drop SMIL and show the element's own attributes, so write what they should show there, with no `fill="freeze"`. `<animateMotion>` adds to the element's place: on a loop, set `cx` and `cy` to the path's start and begin the path at `M0 0`. Or use `offset-path:path('…')` with `offset-distance` keyframes, which a still shows at the line's start, or a one-shot's end with `forwards` or `both`. Fragments with `data-fragment-index` go first, lowest first, then file order. A `transition-delay` on the second of two sharing an index plays it after the first, if it has CSS of its own: with none it gets a fixed fade. Magic move: `data-transition="magic"` on the slide plus the same `id` on the matching absolute element of both slides. A deck with no `format` key has no fragments: there, `data-build-in` / `data-build-out="fade|rise|drop|left|right|scale|pop [order 1–50] [auto]"` on a slide's `position:absolute` children. `auto` steps fire about 600 ms after arrival, then every 520 ms.

**No `format` key.** There lengths are px, colours hex, `rgb()`, `hsl()` or a name, and tags only `h1`–`h3`, `p`, lists, `div`, `img`, `video`, `svg`, `table`, `hr`, `x-*` and, in a line, `b`, `i`, `u`, `a`, `span`, `br`; no em, rem, `var()`, margins, `animation`, `minmax()` or grid line numbers, and `width` and `height` include padding and border. `calc()` works only as `calc(% ± px)` on `left` and `top`, and `%` only on pinned boxes, cells, flex shares, `border-radius` and a -50% translate. Anything else may be dropped; `display` is only `flex`, `grid`, `block` or `none`, and `position:relative` doesn't offset. A `data-flat` slide is the same.

**Limits.** A slide is held read-only, with the reason, past these: malformed markup, a tag that isn't HTML, an unknown `x-shape` kind; over 200 boxes or 15 nested `<div>`s once read; over 24 positioned children in one `<div>`; text over 20000 characters or 100 inline marks; over 4000 table cells or an over-limit table; a slide or its stylesheet too large to read. A deck holds 500 slides. No scripts, no `<iframe>`, `<canvas>` or `<object>` outside `<x-embed>`, no image URLs. Where a build runs, it reports each slide it would hold and what it dropped and why.
