# DevDay 2026 attendee guide

Applies only to Tuesday, September 29, 2026 at Fort Mason Center for Arts & Culture, 2 Marina Boulevard, San Francisco, CA 94123. All times are Pacific Time (America/Los_Angeles).

Curated from the OpenAI DevDay 2026 Event Guide, updated September 27, 2026. This is a dated logistics snapshot. Follow newer official event communications and onsite signage if details change. Use [devday.openai.com](https://devday.openai.com/) for current public information and session listings; this reference does not establish product availability or announcements.

## Arrival and departure

- Registration: 8:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. in Walk in the Park. Complete registration before arriving; tickets are not sold onsite.
- Check the confirmation email for the correct entrance lane. Do not assume eligibility for the Media & Staff Entrance.
- Festival Pavilion doors open at 9:00 a.m.; the opening keynote runs 10:00–10:50 a.m.
- Rideshare is recommended; parking is not recommended. Use the venue address above as the destination, not as an exact check-in entrance.
- Wear both the event badge and wristband throughout the day. The wristband indicates access level; visit the registration help desk for credential questions. Wait until after the event to post badge images.
- Bag drop: Storehouse, Building D, 8:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m. Collect belongings before closing.
- Merchandise pickup: parking lot next to Building C, within Walk in the Park, approximately 11:00 a.m. after the keynote until 7:00 p.m. Have the confirmation email's QR code ready.

## Attendee services

- Accessibility: quiet rooms, lactation rooms, and dietary accommodations are available. Email devday@openai.com for accommodation requests; ask staff for accessible routes and room locations.
- Quiet Room: Gateway Pavilion, Level 1, 11:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m.
- Restrooms: Festival Pavilion and Gateway Pavilion. Gateway opens during breakfast for restroom access only; its activities open at 11:00 a.m.
- Lost and found: registration help desk during registration hours. Proof of ownership is required to collect an item.
- Wi-Fi: network name is OpenAI DevDay. Ask event staff for connection details.
- First Aid: office left of the main entrance to Festival Pavilion. Ask staff or security for assistance. In an emergency, call 911, then notify onsite security and follow their directions.
- General questions: ask event staff or email devday@openai.com.
