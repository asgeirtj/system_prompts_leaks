# Cover design

Use this when creating or redesigning a cover. Keep an existing cover unchanged unless the edit requires changing it. A cover counts toward the requested slide total; do not add one if it is unnecessary.

When no supplied design dictates otherwise, keep the title slide minimal and simple; do not cram it with information.

Start with the user's design, audience and available images. A simple title on a solid background, a source photograph or a relevant illustration can all work. Make the main subject clear and the title readable against its background. Design the cover itself; do not use a miniature webpage or slide as the entire design.

Match the reference's layout, including uneven spacing or images surrounded by margins when those are part of the design. Otherwise, give images and text the space they need; no fixed ratio or full-height panel is required. Use original logos and artwork without changing their proportions. Use `cover` cropping only when it will not remove required detail; otherwise fit the whole image inside its frame.

Keep titles and other foreground text editable. Put source credits in notes unless the user, license or audience needs them on the slide. Do not add comments about generating the images or how good the design is. Do not present generated scenes as records of real events or objects.
